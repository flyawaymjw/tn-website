Clinical Scope & Services

Surgical & Onco-Dermatology

  • Melanoma clinical clearance
  • Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC)
  • Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC)
  • Actinic Keratoses (Pre-cancers)
  • Advanced Mohs Microsurgery

General Medical Dermatology

  • Atypical Moles & Lesions
  • Symptomatic Cysts & Warts
  • Acne & Rosacea management
  • Eczema & Psoriasis therapies
  • Fungal infections (Skin & Nails)
⚠️ Clinical Focus Notice: Woodbury Dermatology is strictly a medical and surgical pathology practice. We operate without the distraction of commercial cosmetic procedures, aesthetic sales, or retail product lines to focus entirely on pathologic disease management.

Michael Woodbury, MD

Solo Practitioner & Medical Director

Born and raised in Memphis, TN, Dr. Woodbury's extensive medical training spans several of the nation's premier institutions. Following his academic pathways, he previously built clinical experience practicing in Greenwich, CT and Silicon Valley. He spent the last two decades serving communities throughout Western Tennessee and Arkansas with a predominant expertise in treating severely sun-damaged skin and its oncological sequelae.

Family has recently brought Dr. Woodbury to warm and sunny Florida, supporting his youngest son as he pursues his dream on local tennis courts. Dr. Woodbury establishes Woodbury Dermatology as a dedicated, traditional solo clinical setting where patients receive direct, un-delegated medical evaluations directly from their physician.
